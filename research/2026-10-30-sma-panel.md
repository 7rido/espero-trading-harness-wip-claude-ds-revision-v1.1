# Exploratory — the paper rule on the panel (2026-10-30)

**Status: exploratory, not pre-registered.** It was run because the paper loop had already been
started without this step; it is a diagnosis, not a test. Any claim that follows needs its own
pre-registration with a criterion fixed before the run.

## What was run

`python -m espero2.cli backtest` — the exact rule the paper books trade (`sma_regime`, 20/50 on
daily closes), on `data/panel` (42 coins, 2021-10-27 → 2026-10-27, 1,826 dates):

- signal at close *i*, fill at open *i+1* (never the signal bar);
- each coin its own $1,000 book, 1× notional, long **or** short;
- taker 4.5 bps + 2 bps slippage per side;
- 1× short liquidated at 1.99× entry (a long at 0.01×), as the paper engine does;
- daily marks; **funding not included** (the panel is daily, funding is hourly);
- portfolio = the average coin book.

## Results

| book | total | CAGR | Sharpe | max DD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **rule, long/short** (what the loop traded) | **+6.3%** | **1.2%** | 0.39 | **−94.6%** |
| **rule, long-only** | **+465%** | **41.4%** | 0.45 | −84.6% |
| BTC buy & hold | +31.2% | 5.6% | 0.36 | −76.6% |
| equal-weight hold of all 42 | +332% | 34.0% | 0.46 | −99.5% |

3,187 trades, $2,519 fees, **13 liquidations** long/short; 1,574 trades, $4,526 fees, 2 liquidations
long-only.

## What it says

1. **The long/short version is worse than holding BTC** — 1.2% a year against 5.6%, with a 95%
   drawdown instead of 77%. The short half is the destructive half: 13 liquidations, and the worst
   coins (ARB, TIA) are shorts that were stopped out by the venue, not by the rule.
2. **Long-only is beta, not alpha.** +465% sounds large, but the equal-weight hold made +332% with
   the same Sharpe (0.45 vs 0.46). The rule's drawdown is smaller (−85% vs −100%) and its return a
   little higher — that is sizing and cash, not forecasting.
3. **Costs matter but are not the story.** $2.5k–4.5k over 42 books of $1,000 is 6–11% of one book's
   stake in fees, spread over five years. The drawdown, not the fee, is what kills it.

## Defects of this run, stated

- **Survivorship**: the panel is today's 42 coins. Coins that died are absent, which flatters every
  long and makes the equal-weight benchmark optimistic.
- **No funding**: a short book's funding is not charged at all here; on the paper engine it is.
- **Daily bars only**: fills at the next open, no intra-day stop path, no slippage by size.
- **Not pre-registered**: the rule, data and criterion were not fixed before it ran. Its numbers can
  motivate a test; they cannot be cited as one.

## Consequence

The short configuration is retired from the paper books. Long-only is the only version that could
justify a pre-registration, and even that needs a criterion that separates it from equal-weight
holding — otherwise it is bet on crypto with extra steps.
